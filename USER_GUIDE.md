## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is DocVerse?

DocVerse is an AI-powered platform that helps you create professional documents including resumes, CVs, cover letters, presentations, and diagrams. All fields are optional, and our AI assists you every step of the way.

### Quick Start (4 Simple Steps)

1. **Choose Your Document Type**

2. **Fill in Your Details**
   - All fields are optional - add what you have

   - Use the helpful tips provided in each section

3. **Generate with AI**
   - Click the "Generate" button
   - AI creates a professional, optimized document
   - Usually takes just a few seconds

4. **Download & Use**
   - Export as PDF, Word, or PowerPoint
   - Download and start using immediately
   - Edit anytime by signing in

## 📝 Creating a Resume

### Guided Resume Builder

Our guided builder walks you through 9 easy steps:

#### Step 1: Personal Information

- **Name, Email, Phone, Location** - Basic contact info
- **Target Role** - The job you're applying for (helps AI optimize)
- **Job Description** - Paste it here for keyword matching
- ✨ **Pro Tip**: Even if you skip fields, our AI can still create a great resume!

#### Step 2: Professional Summary

- Write a brief 3-4 sentence overview
- Mention your experience and key skills
- Align it with your target role
- 💡 **Example**: "Results-driven software engineer with 5+ years of experience in full-stack development..."

#### Step 3: Work Experience

- Add as many positions as you'd like
- **Pro Tips:**
  - Start with action verbs (Led, Developed, Increased)
  - Include numbers and metrics
  - Focus on achievements, not just duties
- Click "+ Add Another Experience" for multiple entries

#### Step 4: Education

- List your degrees and institutions
- Include GPA if 3.5 or higher
- Mention honors and awards
- 💡 **Recent Graduates**: Put education before experience

#### Step 5: Skills

- **Four Categories:**
  - Technical (e.g., Machine Learning, Cloud Architecture)
  - Programming (e.g., Python, React, Node.js)
  - Tools (e.g., Docker, AWS, Git)
  - Soft Skills (e.g., Leadership, Communication)
- **How to Add**: Type and press Enter, or click the + button
- **How to Remove**: Click on any skill tag

#### Step 6: Projects

- Showcase relevant projects
- Include GitHub links or live demos
- Mention technologies used
- Focus on impact and results

#### Step 7: Certifications

- Industry-recognized certifications
- Add credential IDs for verification
- Optional section - skip if you don't have any

#### Step 8: Professional Links

- **LinkedIn**: Most important for networking
- **GitHub**: Essential for developers
- **Portfolio**: Great for designers and creatives
- **Website**: Personal brand showcase

#### Step 9: Review & Generate

- Review all your information
- See what sections you've completed
- Click "Generate ATS-Optimized Resume"
- Wait a few seconds for AI to work its magic!

## 💼 Best Practices

### Resume Writing Tips

#### For Experience Section

✅ **Do:**

- Start with action verbs
- Include specific metrics
- Focus on achievements
- Mention technologies used

❌ **Don't:**

- Use passive voice
- Write long paragraphs
- List only responsibilities
- Forget to quantify results

#### For Skills Section

✅ **Do:**

- List relevant skills for the job
- Organize by category
- Include both technical and soft skills
- Match keywords from job description

❌ **Don't:**

- Add every skill you've ever learned
- Mix categories randomly
- Forget soft skills
- List outdated technologies

### ATS Optimization

**What is ATS?**
Applicant Tracking Systems scan resumes before humans see them. Our AI ensures your resume passes ATS checks.

**How We Help:**

- ✅ Keyword optimization
- ✅ Proper formatting
- ✅ Relevant section headings
- ✅ Clean, parsable structure

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"My resume isn't generating"**

- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you're signed in
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact support if issue persists

**"I can't see my saved documents"**

- Ensure you're signed in to the same account
- Check your profile page
- Clear browser cache and reload

**"The interface looks broken"**

- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
- Update your browser to the latest version
- Disable browser extensions temporarily
- Clear cache and cookies

**"I need help with a specific field"**

- Click the help button (?) for tooltips
- Read the colored guidance boxes
